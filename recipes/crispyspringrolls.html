Crispy Spring Rolls

Description

A deep-fried Liyue snack. Shredded meat and vegetables are wrapped in a thin pancake, rolled into a tube, and then fried until golden. Crunchy on the outside, smooth on the inside, these little delicacies are cherished by old and young alike.


Ingredients

  • Foul (3)
  • Bamboo shoot (2)
  • Mushroom (2)
  • Flour (2)

Effects

  • Increases all party members' DEF by 200 for 300s.
